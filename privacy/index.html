隱私權

生效日期 2026 年 11 月 5 日

這個網站用自己的方式統計瀏覽數據，目的只有一個：知道哪些內容有人讀、讀者從哪裡來，好把網站做得更好。統計不使用 cookie，也不會辨識你是誰。

會記錄什麼

  • 看了哪一頁、捲到多深、頁面停留在前景多少秒，以及是否讀完一篇文章。
  • 從哪個網站連過來（只有網站名稱，例如 facebook.com，不含完整網址），以及連結上的 utm 參數或分享來源。
  • 按了哪個分享按鈕、點了哪個外部網站的連結、切換了哪個語言。
  • 國家或地區（由 Cloudflare 依連線判斷，只到國家）、裝置類型（手機、平板、電腦）、瀏覽器與作業系統的種類、介面語言、深淺色主題。
  • 頁面載入速度（Core Web Vitals）與網頁程式錯誤訊息，用來修網站。

不會記錄什麼

  • 不存 IP 位址、不存完整的瀏覽器識別字串、不設 cookie、不做跨網站追蹤，也不記錄滑鼠軌跡或錄製畫面。
  • 為了估計「有多少不同的人」，伺服器會把當天日期、一組私密金鑰、IP 位址和瀏覽器字串混成一段雜湊碼，只保留這段雜湊碼。同一天同一台裝置會得到同一段，隔天就完全不同，所以無法跨日認出同一個人，也無法反推出 IP。

保存多久、存在哪裡

逐筆的瀏覽紀錄存在 Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine，約 3 個月後自動刪除。之後只會留下每天的合計數字（例如某篇文章某天有幾次瀏覽），這些數字不含任何個人資料。資料只有網站作者看得到，不會出售或提供給他人。

不想被統計

瀏覽器開啟「不要追蹤」（Do Not Track）或「全球隱私控制」（Global Privacy Control）時，這個網站完全不送統計資料。也可以在任何網址後面加上 ?notrack，這台瀏覽器之後就不再被統計；加 ?notrack=0 可以恢復。

其他服務

  • 首頁載入 Google AdSense 的程式，Google 可能使用 cookie 依你的瀏覽紀錄顯示廣告。詳情請見 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站的資訊，也可以到 廣告設定關閉個人化廣告。
  • 字型由 Google Fonts 提供，文章圖片由 Cloudflare 提供，這些服務在傳送檔案時會看到你的連線位址。
  • 部落格文章存在 Google Firebase；只有網站作者會登入，讀者不需要也不能登入。

Privacy

Effective November 5, 2026

This site keeps its own visitor statistics for one reason: to know what people read and how they find it, so the site can get better. The statistics use no cookies and never identify who you are.

What is recorded

  • Which page you viewed, how far you scrolled, how many seconds the page was in front, and whether you finished a post.
  • The site that linked you here (its name only, such as facebook.com, never the full address), and any utm or share tag on the link.
  • Which share button you pressed, which outside site you followed a link to, and language switches.
  • Your country or region (from Cloudflare, country only), device type (phone, tablet, computer), browser and system family, interface language and light or dark theme.
  • Page speed (Core Web Vitals) and script error messages, to fix the site.

What is not recorded

  • No IP address, no full browser string, no cookies, no cross-site tracking, no mouse tracking or session recording.
  • To estimate how many different people visit, the server mixes the date, a private key, your IP address and browser string into a short hash and keeps only that hash. The same device gets the same hash for one day and a completely different one the next, so no one can be recognized across days and the IP address can't be recovered.

How long it is kept, and where

Individual page views are stored in Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine and deleted automatically after about 3 months. After that only daily totals remain (for example, how many views a post had on a day), which contain no personal data. Only the site's author can see the data; it is never sold or shared.

Opting out

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, this site sends no statistics at all. You can also add ?notrack to any address on this site and this browser won't be counted again; ?notrack=0 turns counting back on.

Other services

  • The home page loads Google AdSense, and Google may use cookies to show ads based on your browsing. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services, or turn off personalized ads in Ad Settings.
  • Fonts come from Google Fonts and post pictures from Cloudflare; these services see your connection address when they send the files.
  • Blog posts are stored in Google Firebase. Only the site's author signs in; readers never need to.
